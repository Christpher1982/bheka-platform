# POPIA control map

Status note: this map records the current best-effort mapping between POPIA's conditions
and sections and Bheka's specific features and controls. It is Provisional because it has
not yet been reviewed by the retained attorney or by an external POPIA auditor, and
because several controls are architecturally designed but not yet operating against a
live tenant. Gaps are marked honestly rather than rounded up to "compliant."

## 1. How to read this map

Each row states: the POPIA condition or section, what it requires in one line, the
specific Bheka feature or control that addresses it, the evidence that the control
exists (a document, schema, or process reference — not a restated schema, per the
anti-drift rule in `CANON.md` section 14), and an honest gap assessment.

Gap ratings: **Closed** (control exists and is verifiable today), **Partial** (control
exists but has a known limitation), **Open** (control is designed but not yet built or
verified), **Attorney review pending** (product control likely sufficient, but the legal
conclusion has not been signed off).

## 11. Section 71 — automated decision making

Treated in full in `017_AI_AND_ANALYTICS.md` section 7 and cross-referenced in
`019_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY_FRAMEWORK.md` section 2. Summary control: no model output alone
triggers an adverse employment decision; a human investigator decision always sits between
a Bheka score and any consequence. Gap: **Attorney review pending** on whether this
architecture in fact falls outside section 71(1)'s "solely automated" prohibition.

## 14. Summary gap register

The following gaps are Open as of this document's review date and should be tracked to
closure before general availability, not just before the first pilot customer:

1. Eride's own Information Officer is not yet named or registered (sections 2, 13 above).
2. Transparency notice text is designed for multi-language delivery but not yet
   attorney-drafted for any specific tenant (section 7).
3. Data subject access, correction, and objection request workflows (sections 9, 10) are
   schema-ready but not yet built as an operator-facing workflow.
4. Breach notification draft-generation against the Information Regulator's eServices
   Portal format is not yet built or tested (section 8).
5. SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification and an independent penetration test have not yet been
   obtained (section 8) — see certification cost estimates in
   `Teramind_assessment_and_build_thesis_2026-07-31.md`.
6. Whether behavioural biometrics used for shared-account detection constitute special
   personal information under section 26 is unresolved (section 10).
7. The lawful-processing-basis position (contract vs. legitimate interest vs. consent) is
   not yet a settled attorney-drafted position per customer sector (section 3).
8. The section 71 "solely automated" legal theory underlying the entire AI governance
   model is Provisional pending attorney sign-off (section 11).

None of these gaps block internal development. All of them should be closed, or
explicitly and knowingly accepted as residual risk by a named owner, before the first
production tenant processes real employee data at scale — see `028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md`
for sequencing against the regulatory filing timeline.

## AI implementation constraints

- This document must not be presented to a prospect or auditor as evidence of POPIA
  "compliance" in absolute terms. It is a control map with honestly marked gaps, not a
  compliance certificate.
- Any change to a Bheka feature that affects a row in this map (for example, adding a new
  telemetry type, changing retention defaults, or introducing a new AI model provider)
  must trigger a review of the corresponding row before release.

- Given this document is shown to a prospect, when they ask if Bheka is "POPIA
  compliant," then the honest answer references the specific closed and open items in
  this map, not a blanket yes.
