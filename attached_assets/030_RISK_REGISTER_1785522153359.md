# Risk register

Status note: this register is Provisional and should be reviewed at least monthly during
active development and quarterly thereafter. Likelihood and Impact are each scored Low,
Medium, or High. A risk scored High/High is a standing item on every leadership check-in
until it is mitigated to at least Medium/Medium or explicitly and knowingly accepted by a
named owner.

## 2. Reading the register

Thirty risks are recorded above, exceeding the minimum of 25 requested. The four risks
explicitly named in the task brief are items 1 (Apple entitlement delay), 2 (ECT Act
registration delay), 3 (Rust hiring), and 4 (single-customer concentration) — all scored
High/High, reflecting that they are the most consequential and most likely risks facing
the v1 plan, not just illustrative examples.

**Distribution by category:** Legal/compliance (items 2, 5, 6, 7, 12, 13, 14, 19, 25, 26,
27, 28, 30) is the largest category, consistent with Bheka's positioning as a compliance-
first product where legal risk is closer to the core business than in a typical SaaS
company. Commercial risk (items 4, 8, 9, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24) is the second largest.
Hiring/continuity (items 3, 23) is small in count but disproportionately high in scored
impact.

**Review cadence.** This register should be reviewed at every quarterly roadmap review
(`028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md`), with any High/High item also reviewed at every
leadership check-in until mitigated. New risks discovered during development must be
added here, not tracked informally elsewhere.

## AI implementation constraints

- Do not remove a risk from this register without a documented mitigation outcome; risks
  are downgraded or closed, not silently deleted.
- Do not add a new feature or customer commitment that introduces a new High/High risk
  without it being added to this register before the commitment is made externally.

## Required inputs

- Quarterly review input from the founder, engineering lead, and (once retained) external
  counsel.
- Updated likelihood/impact scores as mitigations are executed and as external conditions
  change (for example, Apple entitlement status, Information Regulator enforcement
  posture).

## Expected outputs

- A quarterly-updated version of this register with closed, downgraded, and newly added
  risks clearly marked with change dates.
- An escalation to the full leadership team for any risk that moves to High/High status
  during a review.

## Dependencies

- `019_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY_FRAMEWORK.md`, `020_POPIA_CONTROL_MAP.md`,
  `021_AFRICA_MODULE.md`, `028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md`, and
  `029_HIRING_AND_TEAM.md` for the underlying detail behind each risk.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a risk is scored High/High, when the leadership team meets, then that risk must
  be an explicit standing agenda item until it is mitigated or knowingly accepted by a
  named owner.
- Given a new material risk is identified during development, when it is discovered, then
  it must be added to this register within the same review cycle, not deferred
  indefinitely.
- Given a mitigation is executed, when this register is next reviewed, then the
  likelihood or impact score must be updated to reflect the mitigation's actual effect,
  not left at its original value.
