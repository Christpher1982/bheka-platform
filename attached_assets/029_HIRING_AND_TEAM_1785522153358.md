## 2. The Rust hiring constraint

This is the single most important staffing risk in the plan and deserves its own
treatment rather than a line in a table.

**Why it matters disproportionately.** The Rust engineer owns `bheka-agent` and
`eride-vault` — the endpoint telemetry collector and the cryptographic key custody
service. Per `028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md`, agent development is the critical path for
the entire v1 timeline: nothing in the backend, console, or compliance module matters if
the agent cannot reliably and securely collect telemetry on Windows, Linux, and
eventually macOS. Unlike the TypeScript roles, there is no realistic path to substituting
a less specialised hire and having them ramp into competence within the v1 timeline —
systems-level programming with ETW, eBPF, and cryptographic protocol implementation
(X25519+HPKE per RFC 9180) is not an entry-level skill.

**Why the South African talent pool is thin.** Rust adoption in South Africa's job market
is materially behind TypeScript, Python, and Java, and the OfferZen salary survey data
used throughout this project's research is itself a TypeScript/JavaScript-dominant
dataset — Rust-specific salary benchmarking for the South African market was not found as
a distinct, well-populated category in the sources consulted for this project, which is
itself evidence of a thin market rather than an oversight in this document's research.
This is marked Open: a Rust-specific South African salary benchmark should be sourced
directly (for example, via a targeted OfferZen or Glassdoor search, or by benchmarking
against systems engineering and cybersecurity roles as a proxy) before finalising a
compensation offer for this role.

**Mitigation options, in order of preference:**

1. **Hire for strong systems-programming fundamentals (C++, Go, or embedded/kernel
   experience) with demonstrated Rust learning ability, rather than requiring years of
   production Rust experience.** The pool of South African engineers with C++ or Go
   systems experience is larger than the pool with production Rust experience, and Rust's
   learning curve from a systems-programming background is materially shorter than from a
   web-development background.
2. **Consider a remote-first search across the African continent or the broader
   Rust-speaking diaspora, rather than restricting the search to South Africa.** This
   trades against the "sovereignty" branding somewhat, but the constitution and CANON
   documents do not require every engineer to be physically in South Africa — only that
   data, keys, and infrastructure reside there.
3. **Pay a premium above the generic senior-developer benchmark for this specific role.**
   Given the disproportionate importance of this role to the critical path, budgeting
   above the R69,019 generic senior figure is justified; the fintech premium seen
   elsewhere in the South African market (R77,645–R107,889 for fintech-specific senior
   roles, per [OfferZen 2026](https://www.offerzen.com/blog/software-developer-salary-south-africa))
   is a reasonable reference point for what a scarce, high-stakes specialism commands.
4. **Consider a contract-to-hire or fractional arrangement for the first 2–3 months** to
   de-risk a hiring mistake on the single most consequential technical role in the
   company, converting to full-time once the working relationship is proven.
5. **Build a bench, not a single point of failure.** Even within a 5-person team, ensure
   at least one other engineer (most plausibly the backend engineer) is deliberately
   cross-trained on enough Rust to review code and provide continuity if the primary Rust
   engineer is unavailable — this is a risk-register item (`030_RISK_REGISTER.md`) as
   well as a hiring one.

**If the Rust hire is not made within the first month**, the entire quarter-by-quarter
plan in `028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md` slips proportionally, since agent development
cannot meaningfully begin without this role filled.

## 3. Salary data used, with sources

The following South African 2026 salary data points were used to construct the table in
section 1 and should be re-verified before making a specific offer, since salary
benchmarks move over time:

- Senior software developer (6–10 years): R69,019/month; 10+ years: R100,232/month; DevOps:
  R45,000–R62,000/month; UX/design: R35,460/month
  ([OfferZen 2026 South African software developer salary survey](https://www.offerzen.com/blog/software-developer-salary-south-africa)).
- Fintech-specific senior developer premium: R77,645–R107,889/month
  ([OfferZen 2026](https://www.offerzen.com/blog/software-developer-salary-south-africa)).
- Sales representative: average R11,995/month, entry R7,946, senior R28,136
  ([Indeed](https://za.indeed.com/career/sales-representative/salaries)); 2026 median
  reported elsewhere at approximately R38,000/month (R460,000/year), range
  R320,000–R650,000/year
  ([BeBee 2026 salary data](https://bebee.com/za/salaries/sales-representative)). These
  two sources diverge meaningfully (roughly 3x), which is itself evidence that South
  African sales compensation varies widely by sector and commission structure and should
  not be treated as a single reliable benchmark.
- Customer success specialist: average reported around R292,622/year (entry R215,281,
  senior R361,505) per SalaryExpert
  ([SalaryExpert](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/customer-success-specialist/south-africa)).
- Customer success manager: average R15,173/month per Indeed
  ([Indeed](https://za.indeed.com/career/customer-success-manager/salaries)); 2026 median
  reported at R570,000/year (R480,000–R660,000/year range) per BeBee
  ([BeBee 2026](https://bebee.com/za/salaries/customer-success-manager)); SaaS-specific
  average R458,663/year per PayScale
  ([PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/research/ZA/Job=Customer_Success_Manager/Salary/748efcc6/Software-as-a-Service-SaaS)).
  As with sales, these sources diverge; the compliance/product generalist role in section
  1 is costed toward the lower-to-middle end of this range given the smaller scope
  relative to a dedicated enterprise customer success manager role.
- Call centre agent: average R94,129/year per PayScale 2026
  ([PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/research/ZA/Job=Call_Center_Agent/Salary)).
- Customer service representative: average R8,983/month per Indeed
  ([Indeed](https://za.indeed.com/career/customer-service-representative/salaries)).

The last two data points (call centre agent, customer service representative) are
included for completeness since they were part of the research gathered for this project,
but they are not directly used in the 5-person v1 team costed in section 1 — v1 does not
budget a dedicated support-desk headcount separate from the compliance/product
generalist role.

## 4. Roles explicitly not in the v1 team

Consistent with keeping the team at 5 people and the critical-path focus on the agent and
core platform, the following roles are deliberately deferred to v2 or later, not
overlooked:

- Dedicated sales headcount — the founder carries sales in v1, consistent with the
  founder-led-sales pattern typical at this stage.
- Dedicated QA/test engineer — testing responsibility is distributed across the
  engineering roles in v1, with the acceptance-criteria and test-checklist discipline in
  every document in this repository (per `CANON.md` section 13) serving as the
  substitute for a dedicated QA function until headcount allows.
- Dedicated security engineer separate from the Rust and DevOps roles — security
  responsibility sits with the Rust engineer (agent and Vault) and DevOps engineer
  (infrastructure) in v1; a dedicated security hire is a v2 consideration, likely timed
  around the SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification push in `028_ROADMAP_AND_MILESTONES.md`
  section 3.
